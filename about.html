Company Overview

SIL Rehab is developed by a company that has secured specialized medical-device investment, earned certification as a venture enterprise, and is driving global growth through exports to the United States — continually working to overcome the limitations of wearable medical devices for dogs. Our core focus is the development of joint braces for small and medium-sized dogs, built on low-durometer, medical-grade silicone. SIL Rehab is a silicone medical device brand dedicated entirely to pets, created to prevent and protect against patellar luxation, hip dysplasia, and a wide range of other joint conditions in dogs.
