SIL Rehab provides tailored protective solutions built on medical-grade silicone technology and specialized expertise to protect your dog’s precious joints.

We are a healthcare brand specializing in researching and developing medical-grade silicone joint braces for pets — especially small and medium-sized dogs prone to joint disease.
It’s especially recommended for small and medium dogs with joint conditions such as patellar luxation or hip issues, or those needing preventive care, as well as highly active dogs that jump frequently.
The core of the SIL Rehab brace is low-durometer medical-grade silicone, which minimizes skin irritation while gently wrapping the joint for stable support and protection.
Using highly flexible, resilient silicone, the brace respects the joint’s natural range of motion and is ergonomically designed to minimize discomfort even during extended wear.
Beyond dogs already diagnosed with a condition, we recommend early preventive use for dogs that frequently climb stairs or live on slippery floors, to help reduce joint strain.
Due to the nature of medical-grade silicone, gently wash with lukewarm water and a neutral detergent, then air-dry away from direct sunlight to maintain hygiene over time.
SIL Rehab collaborates with veterinary clinics and specialists to review our design direction, and pet guardians can use it as a supportive care tool after consulting with their veterinarian.
SIL Rehab aims for a world where dogs can walk and run freely, free from pain and discomfort — setting a new standard for wearable pet medical devices.
