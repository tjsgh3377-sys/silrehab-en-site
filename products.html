How to Use

How to Use & Precautions

📘 How to Use
  • · Position the knee precisely in the front opening of the brace before securing it.
  • · Adjust the fit during use so the brace does not shift medially or laterally.
  • · If the brace slips toward the hips, we recommend pairing it with our anti-slip shirt (sold separately).
  • · Keep the left and right fit symmetrical.
  • · Safe to wear during underwater rehabilitation exercises.
⚠️ Precautions
  • · If your dog has patellar luxation or a spinal condition, consult your veterinarian before use.
  • · Do not stretch or pull the product with excessive force.
  • · Watch your dog continuously to make sure they don’t bite or chew the brace.
  • · Temporary discomfort or altered movement may occur for 5–10 minutes during first-time wear.
  • · If discomfort persists, stop use and consult your veterinarian.
  • · Do not use over skin conditions or surgical incision sites.
